Core Innovations

Automatic Movement

Self-winding mechanical movements engineered with synthetic sapphire jewels for frictionless motion and lifetime accuracy.

Sapphire Crystal Glass

Ultra-durable, scratch-resistant synthetic crystal casing treated with double anti-reflective coating for crystal readability.

Smart Sensor Suite

Advanced optical bio-sensors providing real-time heart rate monitoring, SpO2 telemetry, and precise GPS tracking.

50M Water Resistance

Precision-sealed gaskets combined with screw-down crown systems engineered to withstand water pressures up to 5 ATM.

Solar Dynamic Charging

High-efficiency photovoltaics convert both natural sunlight and indoor light sources into continuous operational power.

Aerospace Titanium

Forged with Grade 5 Titanium alloys to offer maximum strength-to-weight performance and hypoallergenic durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register my watch warranty?
You can register your watch by filling out the online support ticket below with your serial number, found on the case back of your timepiece.
How often should my timepiece be serviced?
We recommend a full overhaul every 3 to 5 years for mechanical watches to preserve mechanical accuracy and water resistance sealings.
What is covered under the international warranty?
Our 5-Year Limited Warranty covers manufacturing defects and internal movement components under standard operating conditions.
