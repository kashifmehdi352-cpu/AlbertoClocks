Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register my watch warranty?
You can register your watch by filling out the online support ticket below with your serial number, found on the case back of your timepiece.
How often should my timepiece be serviced?
We recommend a full overhaul every 3 to 5 years for mechanical watches to preserve mechanical accuracy and water resistance sealings.
What is covered under the international warranty?
Our 5-Year Limited Warranty covers manufacturing defects and internal movement components under standard operating conditions.
